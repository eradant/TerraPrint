Terrain you can hold

TerraPrint turns a patch of real ground into a multi-color 3D print. Elevation from USGS 3DEP, color from satellite imagery, banded into the filaments you actually own.

  1. Pick the ground
    Search a place or drag a box on the map. The box is the print.
  2. Set six knobs
    Size, exaggeration, resolution, base, contours, water. The tray tells you what each one costs in grams and hours.
  3. Fix what the classifier missed
    Paint water back into a river it read as rock, or repaint a color band by hand.
  4. Export to the slicer
    3MF keeps the colors. Optional poster sheet for the wall behind it.
